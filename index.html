The Short Version

Built for the neighborhood.

Lancaster Coffee Co. & Cafe started with a simple idea: your neighborhood deserves a coffee shop that actually feels like the neighborhood. Every cup is pulled fresh, every pastry is baked in-house, and every regular gets to know the person behind the counter.

Whether it's a quick espresso on the way to work or a slow Sunday breakfast, this is a place built to be part of the everyday.
